Mosi-oa-Tunya Charter Services Website Plan

Site Name

Mosi-oa-Tunya Charter Services

Why I chose this name The name Mosi-oa-tunya Charter Services directly represents the business as a tourist aviation business and associates it with the famous Mosi-oa-tunya(Victoria Falls), one of Zimbabwe's most sought after tourist destinations and one of the world's seven wonders. This name is memorable, professional and identifies its target market.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote Mosi-oa-tunya Charter Services by providing information about charter flight services, destinations, aircraft, pricing and online booking options for tourists and business travellers. The website will: - Show service packages -Provide contact information -Allow customers to make bookings online -Allow customers to make enquiries online -Share information about destinations and travel services -Showcase charter aircraft

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1:"We are a family of six travelling from New York, going on vacation to Victoria Falls and Lake Kariba, which package suits us best?"
  • Scenario 2:"I travel regularly to Chiredzi for business, how much does it cost to charter a plane from Harare to Chiredzi?"
